# FreeComplete plan

Build an original, no-subscription macOS autocomplete replacement with Cotypist-level English quality and latency, not merely working feature paths. This is a separate implementation, not a patch or bypass of Cotypist. Josh reconfirms that same-quality replacement is the acceptance criterion and challenges the added Japanese work. English autocomplete is the acceptance focus; multilingual diagnostics are historical observations, not release blockers. The current build does not meet the goal: no complete paired English quality/latency comparison establishes parity. Successful packaging and safety checks do not complete this goal.

### Acceptance checklist


1. **Writing interaction:** verify and refine ghost-text placement, font matching, contrast, clipping and wrapping; word/full acceptance, typed-through continuation, dismissal and normal Tab behavior; stale-suggestion removal after focus, selection or document changes. Gate: what the user sees is what the app inserts, without disrupting ordinary typing in supported text fields.
2. **Native app finish:** review Home, Writing, Apps and Privacy as one coherent macOS app. Tighten hierarchy, spacing, labels and help; make enabled, paused, loading, unsupported and permission-denied states clear; keep important actions visible. Gate: a new user can enable suggestions, understand privacy choices, try the app and recover from missing permission without developer help.
3. **Daily-use reliability:** the immediate-exit Metal assertion is corrected and covered by real-model stop/reload/cancellation checks. Still exercise the app's launch, quit/relaunch, sleep/wake, app switching and permission recovery; confirm excluded and secure inputs stay excluded. Gate: supported daily workflows behave predictably, and the app does not leave suggestions or inference resources behind.
4. **English quality and perceived speed:** use a small repeatable set of real writing tasks plus held-out examples. Check relevance, repeated/placeholder output, instructions and all advertised writing actions. Measure cold start, warm time-to-visible-suggestion and rapid typing on the actual UI. Keep the English quality/latency objective; do not claim Cotypist parity from a build or isolated model result.
5. **Power and battery controls:** add a visible Automatic / Performance / Battery Saver policy, preserving English suggestion quality while changing background work and responsiveness tradeoffs. Automatic should respond to power source and macOS Low Power Mode, with an explicit user override. Review speculative follow-on generation, optional OCR cadence and model residency after inactivity; do not equate resident model memory with active CPU/GPU work. Keep permission/privacy gates independent of power mode. Gate: real idle and typing measurements demonstrate the tradeoff, and the UI explains any extra wake latency without claiming an unmeasured battery-life gain. Resolve safe inference stop/unload behavior before using it for idle resource release.
6. **Savings statistics:** extend the existing lifetime completion/word counters into a visible Home summary with daily/weekly activity and lifetime totals. Count accepted continuation content, not merely displayed suggestions; keep rewrite/replacement activity separate so replacing existing text does not count it all as typing saved. Define partial-word and repeated acceptance accounting before using the current whitespace-split word counter as a release metric. Show completion count and inserted characters as direct metrics; label keystroke/time savings as estimates with a stated, adjustable typing-speed assumption. Store local aggregate counts, not writing content, and provide a manual reset. Gate: totals persist correctly across relaunch and day boundaries, do not double-count acceptances, and distinguish measured counters from estimates.
7. **Public-release readiness:** verify the declared supported apps and shortcuts on a clean user setup; review app identity, versioning, installation, signing/notarization, licenses and privacy/help copy. Gate: public claims match exercised behavior, installation works without the development environment, and remaining limitations are explicit.

Start with one bounded whole-app visual/interaction review and a concrete defect list. Fix functional and misleading-state defects before cosmetic inconsistencies. Preserve the current native design and working behavior. Beyond the two requested saving additions, do not add terminal work, new providers/models or a broad redesign to this release-polish pass.

Historical saving-options research (before the 2026-10-26 implementation): the app preloaded while enabled and started speculative follow-on generation, with no power-source/Low Power Mode policy or idle unload. The old whitespace-based lifetime word counter mixed replacements with continuations. New aggregate statistics deliberately do not convert those legacy values into exact typing-savings metrics.

## Design

- Self-contained `.app` with a pinned open-source llama.cpp helper and the [public Gemma 4 E2B i1-Q4_K_M base GGUF](https://huggingface.co/mradermacher/gemma-4-E2B-i1-GGUF) under Apache-2.0, revision `a9bf638e53783fc93778f357cc5c672eab5393b1`, SHA-256 `3cc0e9c2b4bbffea7f3f6cfe1ffd1591f4dafe02c43f78952f1142a24172df65`. No external Ollama dependency or subscription. Bundle licenses and our original runtime patches. Never ship target code, assets, installed weights or private profiles.
- Original ranked search: at most nine active sequences and top-twenty retained distributions. Reconstruct up to five eligible before-caret source tokens under an exact byte constraint; apply the relative pending-prefix gate and ordinary first-scanned/Float32(0.05) gate in separate phases. Score reconstruction with summed original model log likelihood plus summed post-prefix retained log probabilities. Preserve generated IDs/raw insertion bytes and stop at confirmed linguistic word boundaries. The prefix penalty and delivery policies are original choices, not exact target-score or target-stop parity.
- Observe the focused editable field through macOS Accessibility. Read bounded cursor context, suppress secure/sensitive/unsupported fields, debounce and cancel stale inference, render nonactivating caret-adjacent suggestions, validate focus/text/range again before insertion. Tab accepts a word, a separate shortcut accepts all, Escape dismisses, Option-Tab passes through.
- Prompt from field text, global/app/website instructions (where AX exposes a document URL), opt-in screenshot OCR and clipboard context, plus controllable learned vocabulary. Offer alternatives, selected-word synonyms and correction on demand. History is opt-in, accepted-writing only by default, with a separate opt-in for all monitored writing; short, capped, app/website-scoped, local encrypted, and deletable by the user.
- Settings expose permissions, model/status, global and per-app/website controls, privacy choices, personalization strength, and a real-model test editor. A visible Dock window and FC status item provide access. Do not grant Accessibility or Screen Recording permissions automatically.
